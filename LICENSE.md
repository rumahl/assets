# Licensing

This is a **multi-license** repository. Each file's license is declared by its
path in [`REUSE.toml`](REUSE.toml), with license texts in [`LICENSES/`](LICENSES/).

- First-party assets default to **CC BY-NC-SA 4.0**.
- Third-party assets keep their own license (for example, the wallpapers are
  under the Unsplash License).
- The name and logo are governed by the rumahl trademark policy.

Do not assume a single license for the whole repository; check `REUSE.toml`.
Run `reuse lint` to verify compliance.
